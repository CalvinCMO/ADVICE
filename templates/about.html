About ADVICE

ADVICE is a multi-tenant SaaS platform built for tertiary institutions, connecting students with certified counselors for mental health support, early intervention, and personal development.

🎓

For Students

Confidential access to professional counselors, wellness tracking, and peer support.

👨‍⚕️

For Counselors

Tools to manage sessions, track progress, and respond quickly to students in crisis.

🏫

For Institutions

Institution-wide analytics, compliance tools, and a managed counseling infrastructure.
